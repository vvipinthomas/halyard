# Governance

- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, triage issues and cut releases. The initial maintainer is the project founder (Vantoras).
- **Decisions** are made by lazy consensus on issues or pull requests. If there is disagreement, maintainers vote and a simple majority decides.
- **Becoming a maintainer:** sustained, quality contributions, nominated by an existing maintainer and approved by majority.
- **Security-sensitive changes** (auth, crypto, proxies) need two maintainer approvals.
- **Releases** follow semantic versioning and are recorded in CHANGELOG.md.
